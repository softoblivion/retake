## ⪩ Retake v2.1.1 ⪨

Fixes found in a review of 2.1.0: no crash when a sheet is reopened quickly, no frozen picture after switching videos, and notes that are never lost to a click that also does something else.

---

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **Crash on a quick reopen** — pressing the gear, the key map button or `?` again while the sheet was still closing could end the app. The sheet now simply opens again.
- **Frozen picture after switching videos** — opening a video from the **Recent** menu could leave the previous video's last frame on screen and hide the note editor and the marks until another menu was opened.
- **A click that saves a note and acts** — clicking **Done**, closing the window or picking another video while a note with a marked region was being saved could pack the zip without that note, lose it, or save it into the next video. Every such action now waits for the save.
- **An empty note when another video opens** — the editor no longer stays open over the next video; an empty note is dropped.
- **A note started from a menu** gets the keyboard at once, also when the menu is still closing.
- **A failed frame image** no longer costs the note: the note is saved with its text and mark, and the message says the image could not be saved.
- **Hand-edited notes files** — an image name with characters a file name cannot have no longer stops the video from opening; two notes that name the same image keep it when one of them is moved, retyped or deleted.
- **Done** leaves out a frame image another program holds instead of failing; `Ctrl+E` does nothing while Done is off.
- **A refused move** of a dragged note puts the playhead back on the note.
- **A sheet whose content changes while it closes** still lets the video come back.
