## ⪩ Retake v2.2.0 ⪨

Like and dislike notes beside the change requests, one row of note buttons with their keys from 1 to 6, a window you can resize and maximize, and a calmer, clearer review layout.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Like and dislike notes** — `5` marks something that works and should stay, `6` something that does not work yet. They have their own colours and icons, appear in the list, on the timeline and in the copied text, and can be switched to any other type like the rest.
- **One row of note buttons** — under the video: Fix `1`, Add `2`, Remove `3`, General `4`, Like `5`, Dislike `6`, each with its icon and key. In a narrow window the buttons drop their keys, then their labels.
- **Move a note between its frame and the whole video** — the editor shows the note's timecode and General; a click or `Ctrl+4` switches. A note that becomes general loses its mark.
- **Resize and maximize** — drag the window's edges, use the maximize button beside minimize, double-click the title bar or use Windows Snap. The video gets the room; the size, the place and whether the window was maximized are remembered.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **General notes on `4`** — `4` starts a note about the whole video, and `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+6` change the note being written. `G` no longer starts a note.
- **New layout** — the video and the notes list share the top; the playback controls, the time and the note buttons sit in one bar over the timeline across the whole window.
- **Notes list** — every note shows the icon of its type, its timecode and its type in colour; the heading counts the notes per type. The `+` of the list is now the General button under the video.
- **Note editor** — the place (timecode or General) on the first row, the five types with their icons on the second.
- **Start screen** — recent videos sit on one card with a video tile, a short folder name and their note counts.
- **Copy menu** — **Text for a chat** is now **Text**.
- **Type colours** — labels and icons of the types use a lighter shade on the dark theme and a darker one on the light theme, so they stay readable on both.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **A note button while writing a note** — clicking a note button while another note was being saved could do nothing; the note is now saved first and the new one starts.
- **Narrow windows** — the title bar keeps every button visible; the video name moves aside instead of covering them.

### ⤷ Removed ˎˊ˗

- **Full screen** (`F11`, the title-bar button, a double click on the video) — maximizing the window replaces it.
- **First and last frame buttons** — `Home` and `End` still go to the first and last frame.
