## ⪩ Retake v2.1.0 ⪨

Notes move along the timeline, general notes cover the whole video, **Done** packs the review into one zip, and the copied text reads well in any chat. A larger title bar and text, full screen, right-click menus everywhere, and a calmer video under tooltips and sheets.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Move notes on the timeline** — drag a note's marker to another frame. The video follows the drag, so you see
  where the note lands; a note with a marked region gets a new frame image at its new frame. `Esc` cancels the drag.
  **Move to the playhead** in a note's menu does the same without dragging.
- **General notes** — notes about the whole video rather than a frame ("the music is too loud overall"): `G`, the `+`
  at the top of the notes list, or **New general note** in a menu. They come first in the list, labelled General,
  and in the copied text.
- **Done** — the button in the title bar (`Ctrl+E`) packs the notes as plain text and their frame images into
  `<name>_notes.zip` inside the notes folder. The message that confirms it has **Show**, which selects the zip in
  Explorer, ready to send. Done again replaces it with the current notes.
- **Full screen** — the button in the title bar, `F11` or a double click on the video fills the monitor; the video
  gets the extra room. `F11`, `Esc` or the button return to the window.
- **Right-click menus** — on a note (in the list or on the timeline), on the video, on the timeline and on the notes
  list, with the key of every action shown on its right.
- **Change the type of a note** — the note editor shows the three types; a click or `Ctrl+1` / `Ctrl+2` / `Ctrl+3`
  switches the note being written or edited, and its mark and frame image take the new colour.
- **Version panel** — a click on the version label opens a small panel with the installed version, whether it is the
  latest, when Retake last checked, and **Check for updates**, as in the other apps; a newer version still opens its
  sheet.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Text for a chat** — the copied text (`Ctrl+Shift+C`, **Copy → Text for a chat**) is plain lines without Markdown
  or a summary: the video name, then `0:00:12:07 FIX — logo flickers for two frames`, one note per line. It reads the
  same in Telegram, WhatsApp or an e-mail.
- **One notes file** — the notes folder holds the notes file and the frame images only. The `.md` copy earlier
  versions wrote is no longer made and is removed from the folder the next time the video is opened.
- **Notes need text** — `Enter` on an empty note keeps the editor open and asks for the text; a click elsewhere
  drops an empty new note.
- **Larger title bar and text** — the title bar is taller with the app name larger than the panel headings, its
  buttons are larger, and every text is a step bigger.
- **Open** shows a video icon instead of a folder.
- **Selected note** — the selection in the notes list sits fully inside its row, with a bar in the note's colour,
  and the list keeps room around it when it scrolls.
- **Settings open at once** — the Settings sheet is prepared in advance, the video under it is replaced by a still
  before the sheet appears, and the video returns only after the sheet has closed.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **Blinking video under tooltips** — moving the pointer over the title-bar buttons no longer makes the paused video
  blink: tooltips and note cards float above the video instead of covering it.
- **Clicks on the video** no longer take the keyboard focus away from the window.
