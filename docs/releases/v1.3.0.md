## ⪩ Frame Notes v1.3.0 ⪨

Every timecode on screen now copies itself with a click, the way timecodes do in Premiere Pro, so a note's position can be pasted straight into the AE or Premiere time field.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Click a timecode to copy it** — the current timecode and the duration next to the transport buttons, the
  timecode of each note in the notes list and the one in the note editor are controls now. A click puts the text
  exactly as shown (`0:01:12:05`) on the clipboard and a toast says `Copied 0:01:12:05`. The note is not selected
  and the playhead does not move; a click elsewhere on the row still opens the note. The timecode lights up on
  hover, reads `Click to copy` as its tooltip, and `Enter` or `Space` copies it when it has keyboard focus. A drag
  across the text selects it as before and copies nothing.
