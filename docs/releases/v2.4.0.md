## ⪩ Retake v2.4.0 ⪨

Undo and go to, notes saved from a marked region without typing, finer playback speeds, and a Delete notes that keeps your own files and never deletes anything for good without asking.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Undo** — `Ctrl+Z` brings back the last removed note or reverts the last change to a note, with its frame image; the **Note removed** notice has an Undo button. One edit is one step, even when it moves the note, changes its text and its region at once. The last 100 steps are kept (fewer when many large frame images changed, the oldest going first) until the video is closed, another one is opened or another frame rate is set.
- **Go to** — `Ctrl+G` opens a field under the timecode: type a timecode (drop-frame too) or a frame number as the app shows it, then press `Enter` to jump there.
- **Notes from a region** — drag a rectangle on the video and pick a type (a click, or `1` `2` `3` `5` `6`) to save a note without typing; **Text** or `Enter` writes a text as before. A note without text shows "No text" and can be given one later with `F2`.
- **Playback speed steps** — `[` and `]` step through 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 4x and 8x, while playing or paused; a click on the speed control lists the steps, and the wheel over it steps too. Pause and frame steps stay frame-exact at every speed.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **Delete notes keeps your files and asks before deleting for good** — only the files Retake wrote go, and files of your own in the notes folder stay where they are; the notice counts what went and how many files were kept. The notes go to the Recycle Bin; where they cannot (a drive without a Recycle Bin, a bin set to remove files at once, files larger than the bin), nothing is deleted until you answer **Delete notes permanently?** with **Delete permanently** or **Cancel** (`Esc`), and the notice then says the notes were deleted permanently.
- **Space after a note** — after a note is saved, cancelled, removed or restored, or a marked region is dropped, Space plays and pauses again without a click on the window.
- **Your pictures in a notes folder** — opening a video whose notes come from Frame Notes 1.0 no longer moves pictures that no note uses into the `frames` folder.

### ⤷ Fixed ˎˊ˗

- **A video that fails to play** no longer discards unsaved notes or the note being typed: the note is saved first, and notes that cannot be written ask **Notes are not saved** before the video closes.
- **Notes are not saved asked twice** — closing the window while a video fails to play opens the question once.
- **Keys pressed while a note is saved** — Space and the arrow keys pressed right after a note is saved now run once it is written instead of being lost.
- **A region change that could not be saved** — the frame image now goes back to the picture of the saved note.
- **A frame-rate change that could not be saved** now keeps the old rate and the old image names.
- **Two notes that share a frame image** keep it after a frame-rate change or a new region of one of them.
- **Both reasons shown** — when the frame image and the note both fail to save, the message names both.
- **Closing the window during a frame grab** no longer risks a crash.
- **An invalid path in the recent list** no longer breaks Delete notes.
- **A click in the notes list while a note is saved** is no longer lost.
- **The edit hint** stays inside a narrow video area.
- **A `^` in a note** no longer breaks the Markdown notes file.
- **Page Up and Page Down** work again in the notes list and the text fields.
- **Updates while offline** — a failed re-check no longer drops an update that is ready to install.
