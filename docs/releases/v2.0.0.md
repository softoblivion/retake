## ⪩ Retake v2.0.0 ⪨

Frame Notes is now **Retake**: a new player that opens the formats editors render and deliver and steps through them frame-exactly, a new look with two themes and a Scale setting, and the same keys and the same notes folders as before.

> Frame Notes 1.4.1 updates itself into Retake: its update sheet offers this version, and after **Restart to update** Retake runs from the same file, with the same file name, the recent videos and the window position carried over. Copies older than 1.4.1 do not find this release: download `Retake.exe` once by hand and use it in place of the old exe. Notes folders need no change; Retake reads and writes them exactly as 1.x did.

---

### ⤷ Added ˎˊ˗

- **Professional formats** — HEVC (8 and 10 bit), ProRes, DNxHD / DNxHR, VP9 and AV1 play alongside H.264, in MP4,
  MOV, MKV and WebM, with no codec packs or Windows extensions. The video engine is built into the exe.
- **Exact frames on every codec** — seeking, stepping, scrubbing and the frame of a note are frame-exact on long-GOP
  and intra-frame files at 23.976 to 60 fps, including drop-frame rates and files whose video starts late. The
  frame number shows next to the timecode and copies on a click.
- **J / K / L** — `L` plays and, pressed again, plays at 2x, 4x and 8x; `K` pauses; `J` steps back while held.
- **Two themes** — Crystal Black (dark, the default) and Crystal Sky (light), switched in Settings.
- **Scale** — the whole window and its text at 100, 125, 150, 175 or 200 %, on top of the Windows scaling, in
  Settings under Appearance. A step too large for the screen is unavailable.
- **Settings** — the gear in the title bar (`Ctrl+,`) opens Theme, Scale, Download updates automatically and Author
  name.
- **Frame rate read by the player** — when the file does not state its rate, or the player reads a different one,
  the frame-rate control turns amber and offers the player's rate.

### ⤷ Changed ˎˊ˗

- **New name and look** — the app is called Retake, with its own icon, a glass title bar that holds the video name,
  the frame-rate control and the file actions, and the Sora typeface.
- **Title bar** — Open, Recent, Copy, the key map and the notes-list toggle sit in the title bar; the video name
  opens a menu that shows the video, its notes folder or its frame images in Explorer. The note counts per type head
  the notes list.
- **Note editor** — the note is typed in an editor over the video, beside the marked region; the hint over the frame
  names the keys.
- **Messages** — the error banner closes on a click anywhere on it; toasts stay 3 s (10 s for warnings and errors) and
  close on a click.
- **Updates** — the version label and the update sheet keep their behaviour; a download that finishes while the
  sheet is closed shows a notice with **Restart** that stays until it is clicked. Release asset: `Retake.exe` with
  `SHA256SUMS`.
- **Data folder** — settings, log and updates live in `retake_data\` beside the exe; the video engine is unpacked
  into `retake_data\bin\` on first start.

### ⤷ Removed ˎˊ˗

- **The browser engine** — the Microsoft Edge runtime that 1.x played video in is no longer needed; playback no
  longer depends on what a browser can decode.
- **Playback-based rate detection** — a file of unknown rate is no longer measured while it plays; the rate the
  player reads is offered instead, or one is picked from the frame-rate menu.
